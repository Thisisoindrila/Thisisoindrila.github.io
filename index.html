MIMIC-III · LLM · GNN

Hybrid LLM-GNN Framework for Multi-label Disease Prediction

Constructing a machine learning dataset from MIMIC-III by integrating discharge summaries with multi-label ICD-9 diagnoses, and investigating a hybrid architecture that models clinical text semantics alongside disease co-occurrence relationships.

Ongoing · 2026
02

COMPUTER VISION · EFFICIENT AI

Multiclass Lemon Leaf Disease Detection

Comparing lightweight, residual, densely connected, multi-scale, efficient-scaling, modern CNN, and transformer-based architectures, with Grad-CAM for model interpretation and benchmarking across accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score.

Ongoing · 2026
03

SYSTEMATIC REVIEW · LIGHTWEIGHT DL

Real-Time Road Crack Detection

A PRISMA-based systematic review analyzing more than 100 research papers on lightweight deep learning models for road crack detection, covering YOLO, U-Net, MobileNet, transformers, and hybrid models.

Under review · 2025
04

LOW-RESOURCE NLP · DEEP LEARNING

Bengali Emotion Recognition

Developed a Bengali emotion dataset comprising 4,300 sentences across six emotions and evaluated LSTM, GRU, CNN hybrids, BanglaBERT, mBERT, and DistilBERT. A CNN+GRU hybrid model achieved 92.28% accuracy.

Published · ICCIT 2025
05

MEDICAL IMAGE ANALYSIS · DEEP LEARNING

Soft Tissue Facial Profile Classification

Explored CNN and transfer-learning architectures including VGG16, ResNet50, EfficientNet, and MobileNetV2 for automated soft tissue facial profile classification, with dataset balancing and GAN-based augmentation to improve model performance.

Ongoing · 2026
